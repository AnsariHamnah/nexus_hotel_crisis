# RESQNET Demo & Pitch Materials

## 3-Minute Demo Script

**(0:00 - 0:30) The Problem & The Hook**
"Imagine a fire breaks out on the 4th floor of a 500-room hotel. Right now, evacuation relies on a plastic map on the back of a door and walkie-talkies. It’s chaotic, and people die because they run *towards* the danger. We built RESQNET to fix this."

**(0:30 - 1:15) The Trigger & AI Triage**
"Watch the dashboard. A smart smoke detector trips in Room 412. Instantly, Firebase Cloud Functions intercept the signal. Google’s Gemini API analyzes the threat context, classifies it as a CRITICAL FIRE, and generates the protocol. Within 2 seconds, the dashboard turns red. Notice the notification? It’s a silent vibration. We don't use loud sirens on staff phones—we need them calm."

**(1:15 - 2:00) Dynamic Routing & IoT**
"Standard maps show the nearest exit, but the nearest exit is blocked by fire. Our custom routing engine dynamically calculates the *safest* path. Watch as the manager taps 'Approve'. Instantly, RESQNET pushes this exact route to the Smart TV inside the guest's room, showing them a glowing green path away from the fire. No physical hardware upgrades needed, just software intelligence."

**(2:00 - 2:30) Staff Engine & Responder Link**
"Meanwhile, the manager sees exactly where security guards are on the live indoor map. As housekeeping sweeps floors, they mark rooms as 'Clear' or 'Needs Help'. As the Fire Brigade arrives, the manager generates a secure, 4-hour PIN. The firefighters click the link on their tablets and instantly see the live hotel blueprint, the exact fire location, and which rooms still have people trapped inside."

**(2:30 - 3:00) The Impact**
"RESQNET costs nothing in hardware to deploy. It runs on the screens hotels already have and the phones staff already carry. It's scalable, offline-resilient via local Raspberry Pi fallbacks, and built purely on Google Cloud and Flutter. RESQNET doesn't just manage emergencies; it orchestrates survival. Thank you."

## Pitch Deck Structure
**Slide 1: Title & Hook**
- "RESQNET: Orchestrating Survival."

**Slide 2: The Problem**
- Chaos in large complexes. Static maps are deadly when danger moves.

**Slide 3: The Solution**
- AI Triage + Dynamic Routing + Real-time Staff tracking.

**Slide 4: Technical Architecture**
- Flutter, Firebase, Gemini. Emphasize the "Dumb Frontend, Smart Backend" zero-trust security.

**Slide 5: Live Demo (Video/Live)**
- Run the simulation tool showing the dashboard lighting up.

**Slide 6: Offline Resilience**
- The 3-Tier fallback (Cloud -> LAN -> Device Regex). Huge selling point for judges.

**Slide 7: Business Model & Scalability**
- B2B SaaS. Cheap deployment because it leverages existing Smart TVs and BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) for staff.

**Slide 8: Team & Vision**
- Who we are and what's next.
